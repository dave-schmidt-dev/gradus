# Gradus candidate walkthrough

- Candidate preparation captures the fixed iOS review inventory on a disposable dark-mode iPhone Simulator and writes `walkthrough.html`, `walkthrough-inventory.json`, and nonempty PNGs inside the candidate workspace.
- The inventory includes immediate Reset/Exit outcomes (the shipped controls have no confirmation dialogs), deterministic current/empty/unavailable widget renders, and bounded SpringBoard widget-gallery/add/tap attempts. If a system surface is inaccessible, preparation stops and preserves `*-blocked.log` beside the screenshots; it is not silently omitted.
- David reviews every screen in the self-contained HTML before upload. Record that single explicit acknowledgement with `PYTHONPATH=app python3 -m release_candidate.walkthrough --ledger .release-state/candidate.json --record-owner-review .release-state/candidates/<candidate-id>/walkthrough-owner-review.json --reviewed-by David`; `app/deploy-testflight --attended` validates and bridges it to the central candidate record automatically.
- `app/archive-upload-ios.sh --upload-only` rejects missing, empty, changed, or candidate-mismatched walkthrough evidence before credentials or any Apple mutation.
